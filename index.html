ABOUT THE MARATHON

MORE THAN A RUN.
IT’S A MOVEMENT.

JCI Drug-Free Marathon 2026 is a community-driven initiative to create awareness about the harmful effects of drugs and to inspire a healthier, stronger and brighter Tamil Nadu. Through the power of running, we bring people together for a drug-free society, healthy living and a better tomorrow.
